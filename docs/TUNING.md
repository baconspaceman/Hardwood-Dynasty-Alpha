# Engine tuning parameters

_Generated by `hwd gen-docs`._ Set any of these in a mod's `"tuning"` section.

| Key | Default | Range | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `game.pace_scale` | 1 | 0.5 to 1.5 | Multiplies game tempo. Used to calibrate possessions per game. |
| `game.skill_slope` | 1 | 0.3 to 2 | How much player skill moves shooting percentages (1 = normal). |
| `game.defense_slope` | 1 | 0.2 to 2 | How much defense matters for shot results. |
| `game.usage_exponent` | 3 | 1 to 6 | How concentrated shots are on the best scorers. Higher = more star-driven. |
| `game.three_rate_scale` | 1 | 0 to 2 | Scales how much shooters' skill changes their three-point volume. |
| `game.fatigue_rate` | 1 | 0 to 3 | How fast energy drains while playing. |
| `game.fatigue_penalty` | 1 | 0 to 3 | How much tiredness hurts skills. |
| `game.turnover_scale` | 1 | 0.3 to 2 | Multiplier on turnover frequency. |
| `game.foul_scale` | 1 | 0.3 to 2 | Multiplier on foul frequency (also set by 'Foul frequency' setting). |
| `game.block_rate` | 0.085 | 0 to 0.3 | Share of missed rim shots that are blocked before skill adjustments. |
| `game.steal_share` | 0.5 | 0 to 1 | Share of turnovers that are steals. |
| `game.assist_scale` | 1 | 0.3 to 1.8 | Multiplier on how often baskets are assisted. |
| `game.hot_hand_size` | 0.03 | 0 to 0.1 | Maximum shooting boost/penalty from hot/cold streaks. |
| `game.night_variance` | 0.022 | 0 to 0.08 | Team 'shooting night' randomness, in shooting percentage points. |
| `game.clutch_slope` | 1 | 0 to 3 | How much the Clutch rating matters late in close games. |
| `game.three_point_bonus` | 0 | -0.1 to 0.1 | Flat bonus (or penalty) to every three-point attempt's chance of going in. |
| `game.tech_rate` | 0.0009 | 0 to 0.01 | Technical fouls per possession. |
| `game.flagrant_rate` | 0.0004 | 0 to 0.01 | Flagrant fouls per possession. |
| `game.sub_hysteresis` | 1.6 | 0.3 to 6 | Minutes of imbalance before the coach substitutes. Lower = more substitutions. |
| `game.zone_rim_penalty` | 0.02 | 0 to 0.1 | How much a zone defense hurts rim finishing against it. |
| `game.shot_clock_violation_rate` | 0.05 | 0 to 0.3 | Share of turnovers that are shot-clock violations (when a clock exists). |
