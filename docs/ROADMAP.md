# Roadmap

Done: engine, eras, economy, injuries, progression, playoffs/play-in, draft, free agency, trades,
finance, college, overseas, careers and life sim, storylines, mods, import/export, CLI.

Ideas next (not promised):

- A graphical front end (the library is UI-agnostic).
- Women's pro and college leagues.
- Contract negotiation minigame with counter-offers and sign-and-trade for the user.
- Draft-day trades and a full expansion draft.
- Referee crews, travel and schedule quirks (the in-season tournament as its own competition).
- Player-vs-player relationships and locker-room cliques in the life sim.
- A public library of community mods, year packs and settings files.
